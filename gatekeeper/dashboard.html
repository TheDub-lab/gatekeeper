How this works

The scanner agent reviews your bank feed and inbox and flags waste — duplicate charges, price hikes, trials converting to paid, subscriptions you don't use. But it has no direct power. Every action it wants to take is an ActionRequest evaluated by the safety protocol: binding check → kill switch → scope whitelist → budget limit → approval gate. Only then does it execute. In-scope cancellations run automatically. Disputes and outbound messages are held here for a human decision. Everything is logged to a tamper-evident audit trail. This is bounded autonomy: the agent works while you sleep, but it can never exceed what you authorized.
